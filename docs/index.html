Solver Settings:

Gap: stop once the layout is within this % of the proven maximum.
0 = search until the best layout is proven.

Partly powered plants: a plant may get only part of its fuel, and then boosts that share of the time. Where it shares tiles with other partly powered plants, the worst case is counted. Takes longer (use a larger gap or Stop); the result is never worse than without.
